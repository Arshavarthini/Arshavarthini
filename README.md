## 🔬 About Me

I am a full-time **PhD research scholar at Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Vellore**, working on
**post-quantum cryptography (PQC)** — cryptography designed to stay secure even against future quantum computers.

**Doctoral research:** *Post-Quantum Cryptographic Techniques for Preventing Unauthorized Access and Data Leakage in Intelligence Systems.*

In simple terms: large quantum computers could break today's RSA and elliptic-curve encryption. My work studies how
the new NIST post-quantum standards can protect sensitive intelligence and AI systems from unauthorized access and data leaks,
including on resource-constrained hardware.

## 🧠 Research Interests

- **Post-Quantum Cryptography** — ML-KEM (FIPS 203), ML-DSA (FIPS 204), SLH-DSA (FIPS 205), FN-DSA (Falcon)
- **Quantum Computing** — quantum gates, circuits and simulation with Qiskit
- **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)** — lightweight, TinyML-based detection for edge and embedded devices
- **Hardware security** — efficient PQC and IDS co-design for constrained devices
- **Secure AI & intelligence systems** — access control, data-leakage prevention, quantum-safe key exchange
